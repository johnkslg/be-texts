Beauty Empire marketing system

What it is: a white-labelled GoHighLevel / LeadConnector CRM run by Automated Growth Agency (app.automatedgrowthagency.com), sub-account "Beauty Empire Aesthetics, Tampa FL". Read live on 2026-11-17. Look-only: nothing was changed.

The short version

  • How it works: Facebook ads send people to offer landing pages ($199 underarm laser, $649 unlimited laser, $149 hyperpigmentation and so on). Each lead lands in the CRM, gets an automatic text, and goes into an "HP Power Dialer" calling queue. Virtual assistants (the "VA" calendars) call them and book a consultation.
  • Leads, last 30 days: 94 opportunities: 80 open, 8 lost, 6 won (6.4%). Revenue recorded: $0, because nobody enters deal values.
  • Biggest problem: the follow-up pipeline is stuck. 511 of the 512 open leads in the Appt Setter Pipeline sit in "Day 1 - AM Follow Up". The Day 1 PM through Day 4 stages are empty, even though the "Tag/Stage Update Day 1-7" workflows are published.
  • Only 1 upcoming appointment on the books (Nov 28), against 2,900 contacts.
  • Meta Ads and Google Ads reports say "Connection expired". The Facebook integration shows "Connect" (not connected), so the CRM can't see ad spend or attribute bookings to ads.
  • Paid features not in use: AI agents (none live), Social Planner (no accounts connected), Reputation/reviews (Google Business Profile not connected), Google Analytics (errors).

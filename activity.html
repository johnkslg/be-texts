Is the agency doing its job?

My read of the log as of Nov 17, 2026. They did real work in July and August, then mostly stopped.

  • Ads are not feeding leads. Facebook is disconnected in the account right now (Settings > Integrations shows "Connect"), so Meta lead ads can't flow in. New leads fell from 50 to 80 a week in July to about 5 a week now, with two zero weeks in late Sep and early Oct. Nobody fixed it for weeks. Google is connected, but under the agency's own Google login.
  • Follow-up collapsed. Texts went from about 920 a month to 74 in October and 51 in November. VA bookings went from 20+ a month to 3.
  • Inbound calls are being missed. 22 of 34 calls from customers went unanswered. Those are the warmest leads you get.
  • Outbound calls look thin. GoHighLevel logs 53 minutes of talk in five months. October's 79 calls averaged under 20 seconds. If real calling happens in their Hot Prospector dialer, ask them for those call logs and recordings.
  • Nothing is measured. No appointment is marked "Showed" and opportunity value is $0, so neither you nor they can tell what a lead or a booking is worth.
  • The Black Friday push was an import of 2,267 old contacts on Nov 16 and a text blast, not new ads.

Ask them for: ad spend and leads by campaign per month since June, why Facebook has been disconnected, the dialer call logs, and who answers inbound calls.

What this says

  • Lead flow peaked mid July (80 in one week) and fell steadily to about 5 a week by November. Two weeks in late Sep and early Oct had zero new leads, which matches the Meta and Google Ads connections having expired.
  • VA bookings track the lead flow: 23 in July and 20 in August, then about 3 a month in Sep and Oct. November rebounds to 12, mostly from the Black Friday push.
  • Correction to my earlier note: the dialer is logging calls. At least 295 of the 591 real leads carry an "hp call N" tag and 140 show "callmade", so Hot Prospector calls do happen; I just can't see call recordings or durations from our login.
  • Texting was the agency's main channel: about 920 texts a month in July and August, then 435 in September, 74 in October and 51 so far in November. The Nov 16 Black Friday blast to the 2,267 imported contacts does not show in this report, so it likely went out as a bulk campaign that isn't credited to a user.
  • Calls in GoHighLevel's own log are small: 202 calls and 53 minutes of talk since June. October had the most (79 dialer calls, 75 answered) but those averaged under 20 seconds each, which looks like voicemail drops or quick hang ups. With at least 295 leads tagged "hp call", most real calling probably happens in Hot Prospector's own system, which our login can't see.
  • Incoming calls: 34 in total, 22 of them missed. That's worth asking the agency about, since a missed inbound call is a warm lead.
  • Attendance is not tracked: 112 appointments are still "Confirmed" and none are marked "Showed", so the log can't tell you how many people actually came in. Your own front desk or POS records are the only way to know.
